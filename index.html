What is
Wazobia?

Three of Nigeria's most widely spoken languages, each with its own word for the same instruction: come. Put the words together and you get a name that has stood for the whole country for decades.

  1. Wa Yoruba come
  2. Zo Hausa come
  3. Bia Igbo come

Three languages.
One invitation.
One night.

YorubaHausaIgbo

Three cultural traditions. One shared celebration.

  • Yoruba

    Language, fashion, music and art carried through generations of celebration.

  • Hausa

    A tradition expressed through language, dress, craft, music and community.

  • Igbo

    A heritage shaped by language, artistry, ceremony and the sound of celebration.

And beyond.

Nigeria is bigger than three names.

Hundreds of ethnic groups, languages, traditions and ways of celebrating share this country. Wazobia is where the name comes from — it was never the whole story.
